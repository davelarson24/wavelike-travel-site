Why book Carnival through an advisor

Carnival runs one of the largest fleets in the world, which means a lot of ships, a lot of itineraries, and a lot of ways to overspend on a cabin category you didn't need. We help you compare ships and sailings honestly, so you land on the one that actually matches your trip — family reunion, quick weekend getaway, or first-time cruiser testing the waters.

We also handle the fine print on deposits, final payment dates, and Carnival's Vacation Protection options.

What we help you sort out

  • Which ship and itinerary fits your group — short getaways versus longer Caribbean sailings
  • Cabin category comparisons, from interior rooms to Cloud 9 Spa staterooms
  • Making sense of Carnival's drink packages, Wi-Fi, and dining add-ons before you buy them
  • Group bookings for family reunions, bachelorette trips, and reunions
  • What to do if a sailing date or itinerary changes
