Your Norwegian Cruise Line travel advisor

Same price as booking direct — plus the honest advice on which ship, itinerary, and cabin actually fits your trip.

Why book NCL through an advisor

Norwegian's Freestyle Cruising gives you more flexibility than most lines — no fixed dining times, no assigned tables — but that flexibility also means more decisions. We help you sort through ship classes, itineraries, and the Free at Sea perks package so you're not guessing which combination actually saves you money.

We also watch for the promotions that come and go, and handle the fine print on deposits, final payment dates, and cancellation policies.

What we help you sort out

  • Which ship and itinerary fits your trip — bigger ships with more to do, or smaller ships with more port time
  • Cabin category comparisons, including when a Club Balcony or Haven suite is actually worth the upgrade
  • Making sense of the Free at Sea perks package — drinks, Wi-Fi, dining, and excursion credits
  • Group bookings for family reunions and milestone trips
  • What to do if an itinerary changes or a port gets skipped

Common questions

Is it more expensive to book NCL through an advisor?

No. Norwegian sets one price across booking channels, so working with us costs the same as booking direct.

What is Free at Sea, and is it worth it?

It's Norwegian's bundled perks program — but which perks make sense depends on your itinerary and cabin. We'll walk through what's actually included for your specific sailing.

Can you help with a group cruise booking?

Yes — coordinating cabins, payments, and preferences across a group is one of the things we handle most often.
