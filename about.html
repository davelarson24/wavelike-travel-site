About us

We're Dave and Deanna.

We're a two-person team based just outside Austin, Texas. We help people plan cruises and resort trips, and we take these trips ourselves — which is how we know which questions are worth asking.

Waveline started because booking a vacation had turned into a research project. Somewhere between the comparison tabs and the fine print, the fun goes out of it. We'd rather do that part for you.

How we work

Two people, one trip, no handoffs.

You're never passed to a queue or a stranger with none of your history. Whoever you talk to already knows your booking.

Travel agent

Dave

Dave is Waveline's accredited travel agent, working under Atlas Coast Travel Group's CLIA and IATAN credentials. He handles bookings, supplier relationships, rate and amenity research, and the certification work behind the specialties listed below.

Planning & client care

Deanna

Deanna handles planning and client communication — the trip itineraries, the day-to-day questions, and staying in touch from the first conversation through the trip home. She's been through the same supplier training programs Dave has.
